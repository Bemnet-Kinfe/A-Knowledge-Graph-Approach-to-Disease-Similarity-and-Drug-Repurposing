A Knowledge-Graph Approach to Disease Similarity and Drug Repurposing

Overview Neighborhood Similarity Repurposing
Drug Repurposing Candidates
We first find diseases similar to your query (by shared genes or phenotypes), then highlight drugs that treat those diseases but are not connected to the query disease in PrimeKG.
Repurposing candidates
Bars show drugs that are linked to similar diseases but not to the base disease in PrimeKG. Bar length = number of supporting diseases.
Jaccard similarity (for picking similar diseases) is defined as |shared neighbors| / |neighbors in either disease|, where neighbors are nodes of the selected type (genes/proteins or phenotypes). Drug candidates are those connected to ≥1 similar disease but not to the base disease.
