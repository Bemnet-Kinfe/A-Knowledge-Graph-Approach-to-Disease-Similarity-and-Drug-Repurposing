Jaccard Similarity

Jaccard similarity shows how similar two diseases are based on the neighbors they share.

It looks at all neighbors of the selected type (for example, genes or phenotypes) that are connected to each disease in PrimeKG, and computes:

Jaccard similarity = (number of shared neighbors) ÷ (total number of unique neighbors across both diseases)

A higher Jaccard value means the two diseases share more of the same connections of that type.
